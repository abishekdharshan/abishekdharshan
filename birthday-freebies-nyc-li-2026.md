Deep-researched guide to every birthday freebie worth farming around NYC and Long Island. Programs verified against official terms and 2025–2026 sources wherever possible. Rough haul if you hit the big ones: **$250+ in free stuff.**

## 📎 Fine print that actually matters

- **Enter your real birthday AT signup** — most apps won't let you edit it later, and several ignore late-added birthdays until next cycle.
- **Email opt-in is load-bearing** (Jersey Mike's makes it a hard eligibility condition). Use a real inbox and **check spam Aug 27–30**.
- **Qualifying purchases this week**: Starbucks (1 paid drink), Jersey Mike's (1 sub), Firehouse ($1+, by Aug 22), Auntie Anne's ($1+), Tim Hortons (1 scan). ~$15–20 total unlocks ~$60 of subs/drinks.
- **App-only redemptions**: Taco Bell (app order), Firehouse (app flow), BWW/7-Eleven (app wallet), Chick-fil-A (scan barcode).
- **"Participating locations" is doing heavy lifting in 2026** — call ahead for Denny's especially.
- **Girlfriend strategy**: she eats the BOGOs (Cold Stone, Carvel sundae, Bowlero game), she's your paying guest at QC NY / the aquarium — and if her birthday's coming, tonight's signup spree sets her up too (30-day-rule programs like AMC, IKEA, and Anthropologie need the head start).
